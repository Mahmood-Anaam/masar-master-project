In order to graduate through non-thesis track, each graduate student must successfully complete Research Seminar (IS595) and Project (IS597). 

Research seminar (IS595) provides a demonstration of graduate student’s ability to explore, develop, and organize materials relating to a specific problem or an applied orientation within the IS field. The student must exhibit an ability to pursue research and investigation in a precise and in clear, direct, and concise manner. The research seminar project is based on survey of current topics in a given field of study. 

However, the Project IS597 is a continuation of IS595.It serves the purpose of providing applied skills to the student based on scientific background. That means, graduate project should be focused on implementation, evaluation and testing and learning skills for the student. Graduate faculty plays the role of guiding the student in proposal writing, requirements collection of the research problem, in addition to analysis, design, implementation, testing, evaluation. 

Several topics can be chosen to conduct student work. The graduate faculty is responsible for guiding the student in choosing the research topic based on his/her research interests, enhancing the student's applied skill in information systems arena. A project should be based on some programming implementation and a computer application that provides the student with applied skills. Some projects such as analytical studies that may result in paper publications are also allowed. Topics and projects already completed by the student in other courses must be avoided. The graduate faculty is the best judge for making such decisions before accepting for a project study. 

# **The project process:** 

1. Meet with assigned faculty and ask her/him to advice you on your final project. 

2. Together, decide on what your project will be. 

3. Once you have a general idea of what your project is going to be, develop a simple proposal to submit to Project Graduation Committee (No later than 3<sup>rd</sup> week) 

4. Upon submission of the project proposal, start working hard under guidance of your supervisor. 

5. You will have to meet regularly with your supervisor in order to make progress.

# **Final Report Format for IS595** 

The final report is meant to be a mentored experience and student should expect to work closely with his/her supervisor throughout the term. 

- I- Title page and abstract 

- IIIntroduction (should include the following): 

   - a. Motivation/scope (1-2 pages). 

   - b. Problem statement (< 3 pages) and s ignificance for IS. 

   - c. Importance of the problem, big picture. 

   - d. Objectives: 3 to 5 sentence summary of overall goals of the project. 

- IIILiterature review (20-30 pages) organized from the general to specific, subheadings reflecting internal organization, and may include: theoretical basis, related research, and similar research methods. 

- IVDiscussion and anticipated applications (5-10 pages) 

- V- Conclusion and Future Work (2-3 pages) 

- VIReferences (20-30 references) 

**Final Report Format for IS597** 

The final report should contain the following: 

- I- The IS595 report updated 

- IIThe proposed system, solution or algorithm (15–20 pages) that includes the following: 

   - a. System architecture (If exists) 

   - b. System design (If exists) 

   - c. Solution or proposed algorithm (in detail) 

   - d. Source of data collection and selected data analysis techniques. 

- IIIImplementation (10-15 pages) 

- IVTesting and evaluation (8-12 pages) 

- V- Conclusion and future work (2-5 pages) 

- VIReferences (20-30 references)
