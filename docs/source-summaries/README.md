## Reading and synthesis conventions

Source files and both summaries share basenames. Pre-existing government filenames remain unchanged. A guide and its extract can have separate file-level summaries but one bibliography key. The Arabic DGA parent note incorporates its detailed BCM-extract summary; neither parent-guide note claims a comprehensive synthesis of the broader risk-management division.

Article summaries distinguish question/contribution, method, inputs/data/sample, results actually reported, evaluation, assumptions/limitations, locators and project implications. Arabic preserves those distinctions and numerical cautions. Guidance supplies recommendations/examples rather than effect sizes. Catalogue records supply public scope/edition facts rather than reviewed clauses. Vendor pages establish documented/advertised functions, not independent correctness or operational benefit. No software tenant was tested. ServiceNow's generated overviews were excluded; original bodies supply the calculation/workflow evidence.

## Boundaries for future writing

1. NIST IR 8286D already describes assets inheriting function criticality/priority. DGA has maximum-impact classification and tier-to-RTO examples. ServiceNow documents configurable RTO/RPO calculation. Do not claim universal absence of these concepts or configurable temporal rules.
2. Define the future gap around the precise service-first temporal method, configured policy, traceability, implementation and evaluation. The corpus does not prove universal novelty or superiority.
3. Importance ranking differs from executable recovery precedence, which also requires prerequisites, capacity, resources and incident conditions.
4. Do not interchange MTPD/MTD, RTO, data-loss RPO, operating-level targets and maturity scores. Explain Sahebjamnia's different operating-level use of RPO.
5. Synthetic demonstrations, expert feedback, sensitivity checks and actual incidents support different inferences. Demonstrating operation is not calculation verification or proven recovery benefit.
6. Preserve the reporting inconsistencies identified in the AMM, container-terminal, asset-criticality, propagation, process-reference and self-assessment studies.
7. Unavailable full standards/articles remain unavailable: never turn catalogue/metadata access into invented full-text evidence.

## Citation and reuse

Cite original publications with \cite{key} from references.bib; main.tex already selects IEEEtran. DOI is included where identified. A markdown summary is not the original source. Inspect original material before using an ambiguous table cell, unseen supplement, exact formula or requirement beyond documented coverage.

This is a focused purposive collection, not an exhaustive systematic review or a PRISMA study. Later LaTeX comparative tables belong under tables/ and should be included with \input{}.
