## Functional coverage
The brief describes BIA inventories of critical services, processes, applications, third parties and locations; recovery objectives; continuity/DR/site plans; runbooks and approvals; exercising/scenario analysis; crisis/recovery tracking; emergency notifications; and business-service importance/impact-tolerance assessments. CMDB integration connects assets, dependencies, plans and events. Business requirements and technical recovery capability gaps are presented as information to manage. The offering spans preparedness and operational response, beyond Masar's defined prioritization scope.
