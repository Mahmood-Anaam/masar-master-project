## Detailed summary

Proposes a framework for physical asset criticality analysis based on BIA, so that the continuity of products/services is ensured through the continuity of the assets. A Bayesian network conditions criticality on economic, social, environmental and resilience factors; a normalized expected asset criticality index guides managers. Data come from expert judgment, historical site data, or quantitative risk scenarios. The authors state it is the first study to apply BIA to determine key physical assets as a prerequisite to criticality analysis.
