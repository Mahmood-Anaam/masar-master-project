## Detailed summary

Extends a BCM framework to information systems (ISCM) and validates it with a survey of IT managers and CIOs in large private and public organizations in Finland. Embeddedness of continuity practices is associated with perceived business impacts; organizational alertness and preparedness showed no such direct relation. Social factors such as committed managers and employees reduce negative impacts.
