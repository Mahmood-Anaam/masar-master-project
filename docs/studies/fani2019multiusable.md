# Fani and Subriadi (2019)

**Citation key:** `fani2019multiusable`  
**Type:** Conference paper (open access)  
**DOI / identifier:** 10.1016/j.procs.2019.11.124

## Bibliographic data

Fani, S. V., Subriadi, A. P. (2019). Business Continuity Plan: Examining of Multi-Usable Framework. Procedia Computer Science, 161, 275-282 (Fifth Information Systems International Conference 2019).

## Access level (what was actually read)

Full text read.

## Detailed summary

Tests a BCP framework (8 elements, 38 activities, following Plan-Do-Check-Act, based on ISO 22301:2012 and COBIT 5) in four organizations: an electricity service company, a water service company, a bank, and a manufacturer. The BIA element contains five activities: business process and IT data collection, IT services prioritization, business process prioritization, disruption impact analysis, and recovery time determination.

## Further details recorded

Results: the electricity company applied the framework without change; the manufacturer changed 12 activities and skipped 5, the water company changed 10 and skipped 6, the bank changed 6 and skipped 7. In the BIA element, the organizations changed the order of activities, performing disruption impact analysis first so that its results could determine business process and IT prioritization. Training, testing and review activities were often skipped for cost, time or policy reasons. The authors propose adding budgeting and conclude the framework is usable across organization types after adjustment. Limitation stated by the authors: the modified framework has not yet been implemented.

## Relevance to the project

Direct empirical support for the sequence impact assessment, then priorities, then IT dependencies; and for configurability across organizations.

## Limitations and cautions

Four cases; details of case results withheld by the authors for confidentiality.
