## Detailed summary

Framework based on hybrid fuzzy BWM-TOPSIS for identifying critical physical assets: BIA determines essential products, a physical asset risk assessment matrix assesses asset criticality by likelihood and impact, and a mathematical model estimates asset continuity parameters. Applied to a petrochemical company.
