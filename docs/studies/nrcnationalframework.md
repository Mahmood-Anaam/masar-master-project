## Detailed summary

The business continuity section applies to entities that provide essential services and necessary products and to owners and operators of critical infrastructure. The policy statement (p. 218) asks them to establish business continuity management, perform a business impact analysis that identifies essential services and the processes supporting them and sets MTPD, RTO (and also MBCO and RPO), assess continuity risks, develop strategies and plans, run exercises, monitor performance, and correct and improve. Business continuity is positioned inside the 'resilience cycle' (anticipation and assessment, prevention, business continuity, preparedness, response, recovery; pp. 219-220). The BIA chapter (pp. 231-239) lists nine steps: identify essential services; identify supporting processes and activities; define impact criteria; assess impact over time; define time frames for resuming activities; prioritize services; identify required resources; identify dependencies and interdependencies; prepare a BIA report approved by the competent authority.

## Further details recorded

Impact criteria are entity-defined (examples: financial, legal and regulatory, reputation, beneficiaries, operational, health and safety, contracts), split into internal and external impacts, with a five-level example scale (very low to catastrophic) and example financial thresholds in SAR. MTPD is the longest tolerable interruption after which negative effects become unacceptable; RTO is the acceptable period to return to normal delivery. Figure 4 (p. 235) gives an illustrative RTO table: catastrophic 0-8 hours; high 8-24 hours; medium 1-2 days; low 1 week; very low 2 weeks. Resources listed (p. 234): human resources, financial services, transport and logistics, data, information and communication systems, buildings and facilities, consumables. Dependencies (pp. 238-239): dependency vs interdependency; three types (primary, internal, final/beneficiary) and four categories (physical, digital, geographic, logical).

## Relevance to the project

Primary regulatory basis for the project: confirms time-based multi-criteria impact assessment, MTPD/RTO, service prioritization and dependency identification, and shows that criteria, scales and thresholds are left to each entity.

## Limitations and cautions

Gives no computational procedure from graded impacts to MTPD/RTO, and no rule for the criticality of dependencies. Publication year is not stated in the supplied extract.
