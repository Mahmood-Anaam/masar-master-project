## Detailed summary

Proposes a framework for adaptive e-business continuity management (e-BCM) with steps for BIA, risk assessment and the business continuity plan, implemented and evaluated in three financial organizations. Key finding reported in the abstract: BIA and continual improvement of the BCMS are the driving factors for establishing an adaptive e-BCM.
