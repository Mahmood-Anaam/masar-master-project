## Detailed summary

Presents a decision support methodology, part of the Reactive Disaster and supply chain Risk decision Support System (ReDRiSS), that gives decision makers a robust recommendation on resource allocation under different levels of information availability; applied in a simulation case study of a food retailer in Berlin affected by a pandemic.
