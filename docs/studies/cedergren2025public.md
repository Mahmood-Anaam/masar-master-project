## Detailed summary

Scoping review of public sector BCM and continuity of operations planning: limited but growing scholarly interest, increased by COVID-19; key factors include leadership support, organizational commitment and training; challenges include multi-stakeholder collaboration and resource constraints; maturity often reported as low but improving; calls for research on adaptive strategies.
