# Full-text source collection

This folder contains 14 academic articles and two NIST guidance documents used in Chapter 2. The PDFs are unmodified source documents. Authors and publishers retain their rights. This noncommercial academic collection does not relicense them. Licence information is recorded per item; third-party content may carry separate notices within the original document.

See [manifest.csv](manifest.csv) or [manifest.json](manifest.json) for source URLs, DOI, attribution, retrieval date, size, page count and SHA-256. The bibliographic authors and titles are also preserved in `references.bib`. Read the [critical notes](../study-notes/README.md) for methods, limitations and exact reading scope.

Most articles specify a Creative Commons licence in their PDFs. The Labus licence is supported by the [journal policy](https://www.comsis.org/copyright.php); the 2018 Romanian article by its [publication record](https://reference-global.com/article/10.2478/mmcks-2018-0021); the Podaras copy by [institutional repository rights metadata](http://hdl.handle.net/11025/21488), which specifies CC BY-NC without an independently confirmed version here. NIST reuse information is available in its [copyright policy](https://www.nist.gov/open/license).
