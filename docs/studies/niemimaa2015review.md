## Detailed summary

Narrative review of 83 peer-reviewed papers (2000-2012) across disciplines, identifying four themes: social aspects as enabler, technology as enabler, salience of IS continuity, and models that improve IS continuity. Proposes an integrative framework and identifies research gaps.
