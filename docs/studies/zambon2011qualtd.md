## Detailed summary

Presents the qualitative time dependency (QualTD) model and technique, used with standard risk assessment methods, to assess availability risks via the propagation of availability incidents in an IT architecture; builds on a quantitative time dependency model but uses only qualitative scales; validated on the authentication and authorization system of a large multinational and evaluated against stakeholders' goals.
