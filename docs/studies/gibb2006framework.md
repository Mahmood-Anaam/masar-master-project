Abstract and bibliographic record (University of Strathclyde repository). Full text not available.

## Detailed summary

Notes that enterprises face risks (terrorism, natural disasters, utility failure) and that change (automation, downsizing, re-engineering, outsourcing) can alter risk. Proposes a framework for the design, implementation and monitoring of a BCM programme within the context of an information strategy.
