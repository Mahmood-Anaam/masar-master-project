## Detailed summary

Pre-BIA module: users grade from very low to very high the effect of configured impact types on a service across a configured time scale; the platform shows the maximum impact per time step and calculates MTPD manually or automatically. Automatic criteria: first impact (first time an impact reaches the user's chosen level), first impact average, and first impact of a specific type. Also minimum operating level, a confidence level for the measure, and a resumption deadline with justification.

## Further details recorded

Consolidated Pre-BIAs merge individual assessments made by different departments for the same service: manual, or automatic by average, weighted average, maximum or minimum, with the same or different criteria per impact type; the consolidated MTPD is then calculated.

## Relevance to the project

Closest published parallel to time-dependent derivation of MTPD from graded impacts.

## Limitations and cautions
