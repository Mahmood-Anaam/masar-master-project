## Access level (what was actually read)

Vendor documentation pages (business impact analysis summary, use cases, structured workflows), the ServiceNow Store listing and the vendor product page were read as search excerpts; a third-party partner page was used for the priority-score statement and is labelled as third-party in the chapter.

## Detailed summary

BIA workflow in which the owner assesses impact categories and dependencies; the application calculates RTO, RPO and recovery tiers and dependencies; review and approval by business or IT managers; archived PDF/Word reports. Native integration with the platform CMDB; dependencies can be refreshed on a schedule; dependency maps support recovery planning.

## Further details recorded

Store listing: configurable BIA templates, impact categories, RTO/RPO/recovery tier calculations, dependency assessments, SLAs and notifications. Distinguishes business impact analysis and technical impact analysis.

## Relevance to the project

Commercial example of impact analysis linked to an operational inventory of services and applications.

## Limitations and cautions

Calculation rules are not described in the documentation consulted; features may change between releases.
