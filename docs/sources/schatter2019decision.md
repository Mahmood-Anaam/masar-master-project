## Abstract-supported contribution

The article presents a decision-support methodology within the Reactive Disaster and supply chain Risk decision Support System (ReDRiSS). The abstract states that the method combines scenario techniques, optimization, and decision theory to support resource-allocation decisions under sparse information and dynamic change. It reports a simulation case involving pandemic disruption of a food retail company in Berlin.

## Relevance to the literature review

The work helps distinguish operational, resource-allocation decision support from Masar’s pre-established and consultative role. TDRP derives service criticality, recovery parameters, and relative priorities from approved BIA data; ReDRiSS, as described in the abstract, addresses dynamic allocation under disaster conditions. The source is cited with an explicit abstract-only qualification.
