# Critical reading: paunescu2020functions

DOI: https://doi.org/10.3846/jbem.2020.12205

Local full text: [PDF](../studies/paunescu2020functions.pdf) — 24 PDF pages.

Reading: complete academic article, 16 October 2026.

Complete article including all results and references read. PDCA-based model with survey of 119 Romanian SMEs, data collected October 2017–January 2018 (pp.10–12). Reports 136 initial interviews, unlike 146 questionnaires in the 2018 article; final sample characteristics and period coincide. Sample independence must not be assumed.

In the strategy regression only risk assessment is significant after adjustment; BIA and scope/arrangements are not (pp.14–15). Strategy is associated with response planning. The effectiveness model reports R-squared .819 for response planning, exercising/review and culture (pp.17–18).

Crucially, effectiveness is defined as a composite of those same domains (p.17), so the large fit is partly dependent on construct overlap. It does not show an 81.9% improvement in actual continuity, establish causality or validate a software tool. Reported nonsignificant BIA association does not refute BIA’s planning role; it motivates examining how outputs become decisions. Geographic scope, self-reporting, respondent knowledge and likely shared sample limit transferability (pp.20–21).

Masar implication: preserve the link from BIA to approved targets and procedures and evaluate this directly through task correctness and traceability. Treat training, review and culture as organizational conditions rather than properties guaranteed by software.
